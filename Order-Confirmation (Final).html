Important Order Information

 

Before Installation — You MUST:
  • Verify part numbers on all boxes match what you ordered.
  • Check for any shipping damage. If you see an issue, email photos to info@wheelwiz.ca before installation.
  • Test-fit your product following the steps below.
 

Wheels
  • Open each wheel box and inspect for defects
  • Remove one wheel from your vehicle and perform a test fit. Studs must line up with the bolt pattern & the wheel must sit flat on the hub.
  • Check that nothing contacts the caliper, valve stem, or wheel weights.
  • Spin the wheel by hand to confirm full clearance.
  • If using an installation kit, hand-thread each bolt/lug to confirm correct fitment.
  • Keep all packaging until you confirm everything fits properly.
 

Wheel & Tire Packages
  • Adjust tire pressure to the PSI noted on your vehicle’s placard.
  • Ensure correct tire rotation pattern is followed.
